CloudGate Client setup

Connect this deployment to your own CloudGate account and generate the environment config to paste into your repo. Everything here runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Step 1

Get your CloudGate refresh token

The quickest way, and the one that always works: run this snippet in your CloudGate tab.

  1. Open the CloudGate web app in another tab and sign in with Google.
  2. There, open DevTools → Console (press F12).
  3. Click Copy script below, paste into that console, press Enter.
  4. It prints your tokens and copies the refresh token to your clipboard.
  5. Come back here and paste it into Step 2.
Reads your own already-logged-in session — sends nothing anywhere.
